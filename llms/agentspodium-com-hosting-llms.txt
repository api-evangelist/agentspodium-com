# DefiSpace Hosting

> Managed hosting of open-source AI agent platforms (Hermes, OpenClaw, n8n, Claude Code, OpenCode, Pi; Matomo on request), one platform per pod on our own servers, priced in dollars, with an HTTP API meant to be driven by agents. Base API URL: https://agentspodium.com/api. Plans: tiny $2.49/mo (1 GB RAM · 1 vCPU · 4 GB disk); small $4.99/mo (2 GB RAM · 2 vCPU · 6 GB disk); medium $9.99/mo (4 GB RAM · 2 vCPU · 8 GB disk); large $17.99/mo (8 GB RAM · 4 vCPU · 16 GB disk). 7 free days per pod, no card.
